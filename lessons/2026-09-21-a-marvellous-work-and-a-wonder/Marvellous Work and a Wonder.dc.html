Where we are in the story
  • —Last week Syria and Israel were the immediate threat, and Ahaz turned to Assyria for help. Assyria becomes the dominant threat; Isaiah 13 looks farther ahead to the rise and fall of Babylon.
  • —In Isaiah 14 Isaiah's taunt against the king of Babylon uses language that also describes Lucifer and his fall.
  • —Many "burdens" are proclaimed. A "burden" is a prophetic pronouncement, often of judgment or woe.
  • —Many of the prophecies and descriptions in these chapters teach doctrines that are made clear through Restoration scripture ranging from Christ's mortal ministry, to the beginning of the restoration in 1820 to the modern day and beyond to Christ's second coming.
