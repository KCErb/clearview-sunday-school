Zedekiah in Jeremiah and Ezekiel
Jeremiah 34:3
Zedekiah will see the king of Babylon face-to-face and be taken to Babylon.
Ezekiel 12:13
Zedekiah will be taken to Babylon, but will not see the land, although he will die there.
The resolution appears in 2 Kings 25:6–7
Zedekiah was captured and brought before Nebuchadnezzar. His sons were killed before his eyes, then he was blinded and taken to Babylon.
Thus, both predictions could be fulfilled literally.
Source: BYU Religious Studies Center
