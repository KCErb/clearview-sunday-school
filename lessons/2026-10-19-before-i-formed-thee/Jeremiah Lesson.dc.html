Opening activity

What words come to mind when you hear the word “Sabbath”?

Discussion

What are some ways we can “hallow the Sabbath day”?

Metaphors in scripture

The book of Jeremiah is rich with metaphor: the partridge, the tree by a river, hunters and fishers, and the potter’s clay (Jeremiah 16–18).

  • What are some of your favorite metaphors in scripture?
  • Are there any in Jeremiah that you discovered this week or have always loved?
The weight of our callings

Jeremiah 1:5 teaches that before he was born, Jeremiah was ordained to be a prophet to the nations. In Jeremiah 20, he laments the difficulties of his calling.

  • What are some things that make our callings difficult?
The weight of our callings
  • Think of a time when fulfilling a responsibility or calling—in the Church, at home, or at work—was difficult.
  • What counsel would you give someone feeling the weight of such a calling, based on what you’ve learned from the Lord?
“I will heal your backslidings”

In Jeremiah 7, the Lord calls His people to repent. In Jeremiah 3:22, He promises, “I will heal your backslidings.”

  • How can we help someone know they are loved, even when they’ve made mistakes?
  • Why can this be difficult sometimes, especially in our closest relationships?
Closing invitation · Fishers and hunters

Prayerfully consider how you might be a fisher or a hunter this week (Jeremiah 16:16).

The Lord may inspire you to make a phone call or share something on social media that builds, lifts, edifies, or inspires.

Act on that prompting and record your experience.
