Choose a question that interests you
01

The Intercessor

What impresses you about the description of the “intercessor” in Isaiah 59:16–21? How does the Savior overcome the conditions described in verses 9–15?

02

Personal Redemption

When have you seen the Lord comfort you? When has He seen value in you and paid the price so that He could restore you?

03

Repairing the Breach

Is there a relationship in your life that seems broken and beyond repair? What do you think the Lord sees in that relationship? Can He redeem and restore it? Can you contribute to repairing “the breach” (Isaiah 58:12)?

This week

Prayerfully consider the value that you and those around you must have, considering the price the Savior paid.

How can you participate in His work of comfort, redemption, and restoration? (In your own life, in the lives of others, and in your relationships with them.)
