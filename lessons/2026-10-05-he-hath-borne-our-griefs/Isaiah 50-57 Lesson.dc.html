01

Isaiah 54:4–10; 57:15–19 provide reassurance and encouragement when we feel distant from the Lord because of our sins or weaknesses. What difference does it make in your life to know these things about Him?

02

Isaiah describes God as both angry with His people and eager to heal them. How do you reconcile those two descriptions?

03

Isaiah 57:18–21 - What do these verses teach us about God's attitude toward those who have sinned? How does understanding God's attitude toward sinners change the way we understand repentance?

Closing invitation
