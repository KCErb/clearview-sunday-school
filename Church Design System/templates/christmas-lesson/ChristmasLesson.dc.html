Where we are in the story
BEFORE

A decree from Caesar sends Joseph and Mary from Nazareth to Bethlehem.

HERE

Christ is born and laid in a manger; an angel tells shepherds keeping watch.

AFTER

The shepherds make it widely known, and Mary keeps these things in her heart.
